About (English)

「曉晴・AI 數據解碼」 is a Traditional Chinese YouTube channel that summarises the US stock market after each close and explains which stocks an AI scoring model ranks highest, and why. The presenter, 曉晴 (Sunny), is an AI-generated virtual anchor. The channel uses a small internal tool, Sunny Video Uploader, to upload and manage its own videos through the YouTube API Services; see the privacy policy.
